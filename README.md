# The Final Hour

Version: Alpha 0.1
Projected Timeline: 2026

Overview:
The world stands on the brink. Nations crumble under political instability, global energy crises fuel conflicts, and artificial intelligence rises as a decisive force in both industry and war. The Final Hour is a Hearts of Iron IV mod set in an alternative near-future, where players must navigate a volatile world order and shape the destiny of their nation.

In this world, democracies teeter, old alliances shatter, and new powers emerge from the chaos. Will you survive the final hour, or will history rewrite itself in fire and uncertainty?

Scenario Highlights 
* Conflict in Ukraine: Escalating tensions spark regional warfare with international implications.
* War in Iran: A fragile Middle East descends into armed conflict.
* Global Energy Crisis: Oil shortages and volatile markets destabilize economies worldwide.
* Rise of Artificial Intelligence: AI technologies influence military strategies, industry, and politics.
* Fragile Democracies: Populism and internal strife challenge the stability of democratic nations.

#   Playable Nations (Alpha Stage)
1. France (FRA)
* Navigate political instability in a fragile democracy.
* Manage economic and energy crises while modernizing the military.
* Respond to rising threats across Europe and beyond.
2. Germany (GER)
* Handle internal political tensions and AI-driven industrial changes.
* Rebuild strategic alliances and assert influence over Europe.
* Balance economic modernization with military preparedness.

Additional nations will be introduced gradually, each with unique events and challenges reflecting the 2026 global landscape.
